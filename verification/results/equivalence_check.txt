==============================================================================
A2b. Analytic ground truth: x[i] = i mod 2
==============================================================================
With a 0/1 sawtooth input the two gathered samples are exactly 0 and 1, so
linear interpolation at position `index + f` returns exactly `f` (even index)
or `1 - f` (odd index). The output is O(1) while the positions are large, so
this isolates the error in the sub-sample fraction. Positions are computed
here in exact integer arithmetic and the reference depends on neither
implementation. Offsets are drawn so that no tap sits on the clamp.
